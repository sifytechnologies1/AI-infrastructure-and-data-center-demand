## 🎯 What This Is

A working notebook, published in public. The essays here are an attempt to think clearly about questions that don't have clean answers:

- Where will AI workloads actually run as power becomes the binding constraint?
- What does "hybrid cloud" mean once you take the marketing language out of it?
- How do data sovereignty rules reshape architecture decisions in practice?
- What do the next ten years of enterprise infrastructure look like as hyperscaler dominance reaches its limits?

Each essay is researched, edited, and meant to stand on its own. The goal is fewer pieces, better thought through, rather than a high-volume publishing schedule.
